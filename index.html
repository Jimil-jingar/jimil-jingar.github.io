FairCKD: Fairness-Aware Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis

        
Diagnosis models can look accurate overall while missing far more cases in some patient groups. A standard XGBoost model caught 85% of CKD cases in adults over 60 but only 32% in adults under 60. FairCKD finds these gaps, measures them with five fairness metrics, reduces them, and explains each prediction with SHAP and LIME.

        

          
5,454patients analysed

          
7ML models compared

          
7 / 7experiments, bias reduced
